## Comprehensive Recommended Tracks Guide

This section provides a thorough compilation of recommended tracks organized by genre and purpose for practical DJ use. Each track includes BPM, key, characteristics, and mixing tips, structured for use in actual DJ sets.

### How to Use This Guide

- **BPM Range**: Understand the typical BPM range for each genre to use as a reference for set construction
- **Key Notation**: Uses the Camelot Wheel system, notated as number + A/B
- **Difficulty**: Rated on a 5-level scale from ★ (beginner) to ★★★★★ (advanced)
- **Mixing Points**: Briefly describes where in each track mixing is most effective

## Mixing Tutorial Tracks - By Transition Technique

Specific track pairings and procedures for learning each transition technique in practice.

### Technique 1: Long Blend (32-64 bars)

**Recommended Pair**: Recondite - Placid → Tale Of Us - Another Earth

Procedure:
1. When Placid's outro begins, cue in Another Earth with the fader at 0
2. Match BPM (122 BPM) and loop Another Earth's kick intro section
3. Over 32 bars, gradually raise Another Earth's EQ Low from 0
4. Simultaneously lower Placid's EQ Low
5. Cross the Mids and Highs gradually in the middle section
6. Bring Placid's fader to 0 in the final 8 bars

**Why this pair**: Both tracks are melodic techno with close BPMs (122 BPM) and compatible keys. The pad overlap creates beautiful harmonies.

### Technique 2: Cut Mix (Instant Switch)

**Recommended Pair**: Charlotte de Witte - Selected → Amelie Lens - Exhale

Procedure:
1. As Selected enters the breakdown, have Exhale standing by at its cue point
2. At the exact moment of Selected's drop (beat 1), instantly switch the fader to Exhale
3. Pre-set Exhale's Low/Mid/High EQ to match the next track
4. After the cut, fine-tune the EQ as needed

**Why this pair**: Both tracks are hard techno with similar breakdown-to-drop structures. Easy to maintain energy levels.

### Technique 3: Acapella Layer

**Recommended Pair**: Robin S - Show Me Love (Instrumental) + CamelPhat & Elderbrook - Cola (Acapella)

Procedure:
1. Play the instrumental version of Show Me Love
2. Layer Cola's acapella with key and tempo matched
3. Lower the mid-highs of Show Me Love's EQ slightly to create space for the acapella
4. After layering the acapella for 8-16 bars, swap to the full version of Cola

### Technique 4: Echo Out

**Recommended Pair**: Ben Klock - Subzero → DVS1 - Black Russian

Procedure:
1. Turn on the echo/delay effect during the last phrase of Subzero
2. Raise the echo feedback while lowering the fader
3. While the echo is playing, fade in Black Russian with the Low cut
4. Bring in Black Russian's Low at the timing when the echo fades out

### Technique 5: Backspin

**Recommended Pair**: Pendulum - Tarantula → Noisia - Could This Be

Procedure:
1. Backspin during Tarantula's break section
2. Fade in Could This Be while the backspin sound is playing
3. Creates a dramatic transition between high-energy DnB tracks
